    How to Apply Your Icon in macOS 26

    1

    Replace any Mac App Icon via Finder

    Export your icon as .icns, open Applications in Finder, select any app, and press ⌘I (Get Info). Drag your downloaded .icns file directly onto the small app icon in the top-left corner of the Info window.

    2

    Use in Xcode or Icon Composer

    Export .iconset to download a complete 10-size folder ready for Xcode Asset Catalogs or iconutil -c icns, or export a 1024×1024 PNG to drop directly into Apple’s Icon Composer.

    3

    macOS 26 HIG Spec Checklist

    Your icon is automatically composed inside an 824×824 px continuous-curvature squircle (R 185.4 px) centered on a 1024×1024 px canvas with a 100 px margin and Apple’s official Y+12 / Blur 28 drop shadow—so macOS 26 will never force it into an automatic gray container.
